## What should I look for in a travel operator?

Look at how they behave before you pay, because that is the best preview of how the trip will go.

- **Do they ask questions?** An operator who asks about group size, dates, pickup and luggage is trying to fit the vehicle to you. One who quotes immediately without asking is guessing.
- **Do they say no when no is right?** A good operator will tell you if a smaller vehicle suits you better, or if your plan is too packed.
- **Do they put the details in writing?** Inclusions, timing and terms should be in a message you can scroll back to.
- **Are they reachable?** Notice how long replies take while you are still just an enquiry.

## How do I check that the vehicle is what I am promised?

Ask for specifics, and do not accept "big van" as an answer.

1. **The vehicle type and model**, not just a photo.
2. **Seating for your exact group** with luggage.
3. **The vehicle number** a day before the trip, so the right one can be recognised at pickup.
4. **The driver's name and phone number**, also before the trip.

We avoid quoting seat counts or features in this article because they depend on the vehicle confirmed for your date. If you ask us, we will answer for your booking in writing.

## What documents and details should I ask about?

Ask the operator to confirm that the vehicle is properly registered, insured and permitted for passenger hire. You do not need to be an expert; the point is that a reliable operator answers calmly and does not dodge. Keep the reply. If something about the answer feels vague, treat that as information.

## What should the quotation contain?

- The **total amount** and what it covers.
- What is **extra** — tolls, parking, night halt, waiting time, permits.
- The **pickup and drop timings**.
- The **advance** required and when the balance is due.
- The **cancellation** terms.

## What are the warning signs?

- Rates that sound far below everyone else's and then grow through the conversation.
- Reluctance to confirm the vehicle or driver until the day.
- Pressure to pay a large advance immediately, without written terms.
- No clear answer on what happens if the schedule changes.
- Photos that look unrelated to the actual vehicle.

None of these proves anything alone, but two or three together are a reason to look elsewhere.

## Should I choose the nearest operator?

Being based locally helps in practical ways: pickup is simpler, they know the roads and the festival-day traffic, and you can speak to someone directly. It does not replace the checks above. Choose on clarity and honesty first, proximity second.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find reliable travels in Dindigul?
Compare how clearly they answer, whether they confirm details in writing and whether the vehicle matches what they promised. Ask the same questions of each operator.

### Should I pay an advance?
It is common to pay one, but get the amount, the balance date and the cancellation terms in writing first.

### Can I see the vehicle before booking?
Ask. You can always ask for recent photos of the actual vehicle and the vehicle details before the trip.

### Is the cheapest quote the best choice?
Not necessarily. Compare what is included, because a low figure that leaves out tolls, parking or waiting time can end up higher.
