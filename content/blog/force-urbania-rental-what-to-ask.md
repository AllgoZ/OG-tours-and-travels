Booking a vehicle for a group is simpler when you ask the right questions up front. A clear conversation before you book means an accurate quote, no surprises on the day, and a vehicle that actually fits your trip. Here is what is worth asking.

## Ask about the trip, not just the price

A price only makes sense in the context of your trip. Before anyone can give you an accurate quotation, they will usually need to know:

- Your **pickup location and destination**.
- Your **travel dates** and roughly how long you need the vehicle.
- The **number of passengers** and how much luggage you expect.
- Any **extra stops** along the way.

Share these clearly and you will get a far more accurate answer than asking "how much for the Urbania?" with no details.

## Questions about comfort and space

Every group is different, so ask what matters for yours:

- Is there comfortable space for your full group on a longer journey?
- Where will the luggage go, and is there enough room for it?
- Is the vehicle suitable for the youngest and oldest travellers in your group?

If anyone quotes you an exact seating or luggage figure without knowing your trip, treat it with caution — the honest answer is that it depends on your specific group and journey, and should be confirmed directly.

## Questions about the journey itself

- Who will be driving, and are they familiar with the route?
- What time will the vehicle reach the pickup point?
- How are tolls, parking and any overnight arrangements handled?

> The goal of these questions is simple: no surprises on the day of travel.

## How pricing usually works

For most trips, the final price depends on the details — the route, the dates, how long you need the vehicle and the nature of the journey. That is why a quotation comes after you share your plan, not before. Be wary of a fixed "one price fits all" figure quoted before anyone understands your trip.

## A quick checklist before you confirm

- Pickup and drop-off points are clear and agreed.
- Dates, timings and duration are confirmed in writing.
- The quotation reflects your actual trip details.
- You know who to contact on the day of travel.
