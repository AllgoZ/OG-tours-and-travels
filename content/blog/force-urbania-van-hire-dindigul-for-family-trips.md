## What does Force Urbania van hire mean for a family?

It means you hire the whole vehicle, with a driver, for your own family's use for a day or longer. Nobody else boards. The vehicle goes where your plan goes — a pickup outside your home, stops that you choose, a return when you decide. That is different from a bus seat or a shared taxi, and it is why the arrangement suits families with children and older members: the pace is yours.

The Force Urbania is a large passenger van from Force Motors, built for carrying groups on long journeys. You can see our vehicle in the [vehicle section of the home page](/#vehicle), and [why a Force Urbania suits a family trip](/blog/why-choose-force-urbania-for-family-trip) explains its strengths and where it is *not* the right choice.

## Which Dindigul families is it a good fit for?

- **Joint families and several couples travelling together**, where splitting into cars would split the conversation too.
- **Families with older members** who prefer to board once, sit comfortably and not change vehicles at every stop.
- **Families with young children**, where having your own space for snacks, spare clothes and a sleeping child makes a long day easier.
- **Families with a lot of luggage**, such as a function trip or a pilgrimage with prayer items.

If the group is a team, a class or a club rather than a family, read [office, college and club outings from Dindigul](/blog/urbania-for-office-and-college-outings-dindigul) instead.

It is a poor fit for two or three people. A car is simpler and cheaper for that, and we will say so honestly if you enquire.

## How should I describe my family when I enquire?

The more specific your message, the more useful the reply. Send:

1. **How many people travel on the day**, written as adults, children and elders — not "up to".
2. **The pickup point in Dindigul.** A landmark or a map pin is enough.
3. **The date, the start time and the return plan.**
4. **The places you want to visit**, even roughly.
5. **Luggage**, such as a couple of bags or full suitcases.
6. **Anything that affects the ride**: a wheelchair, a member who feels sick on hill roads, a baby who needs frequent breaks.

If you would rather not type it all, the trip form in the [enquiry section](/#enquire) prepares a WhatsApp message for you. It opens WhatsApp; it does not send anything on its own.

## What should the quotation include?

Ask for these in writing, whichever operator you speak to:

- What is **included** and what is **extra** — tolls, parking, night halt, waiting time.
- **Pickup and drop timings** and what happens if the schedule slips on the day.
- **Seating and luggage space** for your group size.
- The **driver's name and contact number**, shared before the trip.
- The **advance and cancellation** terms.
