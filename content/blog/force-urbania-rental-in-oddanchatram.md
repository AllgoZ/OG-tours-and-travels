## Which trips start from Oddanchatram?

Oddanchatram's location makes it a natural starting point for these journeys:

- **Palani** — roughly 28 km away. The Murugan temple on the hill is the reason most groups go, and mornings are the usual choice because of queues and heat.
- **Kodaikanal** — roughly 90 km, a good part of it on hill roads, which is why it needs more planning than the distance suggests. Our [Oddanchatram to Kodaikanal group travel guide](/blog/oddanchatram-to-kodaikanal-group-travel-guide) covers the route, timing and stops.

- **Combined temple and hill trips** — see [Oddanchatram to Palani and Kodaikanal: a group trip guide](/blog/oddanchatram-palani-kodaikanal-group-trip) if you want both in one plan.

2. **Send the details** — group size, pickup landmark in Oddanchatram, dates, destinations, luggage and any special needs.
