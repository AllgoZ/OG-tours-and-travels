Around Oddanchatram, Palani and Dindigul, a large share of group travel is not holidays at all — it is **weddings, family functions and temple visits**. These trips share one challenge: many people who must arrive at the same place, at the same time, usually early in the morning. A little planning makes all the difference.

*Distances are approximate and from public travel sources: Oddanchatram–Palani roughly 28 km, Oddanchatram–Dindigul roughly 29 km. Check your route on a map.*

## Plan around the muhurtham, not the other way round

Wedding and function timings are fixed. Work backwards from them:

1. Note the **ceremony time** and the **time guests must be seated**.
2. Add the **travel time** from each pickup point, then add a generous buffer for traffic, parking and unexpected delays.
3. Agree one **pickup point and time** with the group and share it in writing.

A vehicle that is waiting and ready is better than guests searching for lifts at the last minute.

## Temple trips: Palani and nearby

Many families combine a function with a temple visit — most often to **Palani**, and to temples around **Dindigul**. Some tips:

- **Start early** — queues build up and the day gets hot.
- **Expect festival crowds.** During festival periods the roads, especially toward Palani, are crowded with pilgrims, some of them walking along the road. Drive carefully and allow much more time.
- **Check timings and dress rules** in advance and tell every member of the group.
- **Agree a meeting point** for after darshan so the group doesn't scatter.

## Keeping guests together

- **One vehicle for one family group** means one arrival, not five.
- **Plan drop-off and pickup spots** near the hall or temple entrance, especially for elderly guests and children.
- **Plan luggage.** Guests travelling from out of town arrive with bags; confirm space beforehand.
- **Share a contact.** One person from your side should be the point of contact for the driver and the schedule.

For most family groups, yes: the group arrives together, parking is simpler and nobody is left behind.
