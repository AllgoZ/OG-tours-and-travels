The moment before you pay for a vehicle is the cheapest time to find a problem. Once the advance is paid and the family has been told the date, a misunderstanding about seats, tolls or timing becomes an argument. This checklist is for anyone about to make an **Urbania booking** — with **OG Tours and Travels** or any other operator. It covers what to confirm about the trip, the vehicle, the cost and the terms, so that your **Force Urbania van hire** goes the way you planned.

*Checklists are general guidance, not a statement of OG Tours and Travels' terms. We confirm your trip's inclusions and terms in writing when you enquire; we do not publish prices or fixed packages on this site.*

## What should I check before booking a Force Urbania?

### 1. Is the trip described in writing?
Write down — and have the operator repeat back — the **date**, **pickup point and time**, **drop point**, **route and stops**, **number of days** and **number of travellers**. A booking by phone alone is easy to misremember.

### 2. Does the seating fit your group?
Ask for the seating of the actual vehicle for your date, then compare it with your group including children and with a margin for comfort. Do not assume; confirm.

### 3. Is there room for luggage?
Say how many bags you expect. If you are carrying something unusual — prayer items, function supplies, folding chairs — mention it now.

### 4. What exactly does the quotation include?
This is where most surprises come from. Ask, line by line:

- fuel,
- tolls and permits,
- parking,
- driver's expenses and night halt,
- waiting time at stops,
- hill-road or distance conditions,
- GST or taxes if applicable.

And ask what happens if the trip goes beyond the agreed distance or time.

### 5. When and how is payment made?
Confirm the **advance amount**, **balance timing**, accepted **payment methods** and whether you will receive a **written confirmation**.

### 6. What is the cancellation and change policy?
Ask what happens if you cancel, reschedule or change the pickup time. Get it in writing before you pay.

### 7. Who is the driver, and how will you reach them?
Ask for the driver's and the operator's phone numbers a day before the trip, so that pickup on the day is smooth.

### 8. What do you know about the vehicle?
You can ask to see recent photographs of the actual vehicle. Our photographs are in the [vehicle section](/#vehicle). Ask about features that matter to your group — air conditioning, seat comfort, charging points, luggage — and have them confirmed rather than assumed. We do not list features here that we have not confirmed for your booking.

### 9. What are the vehicle's papers and the rules for your route?
Ask whether the vehicle is permitted for your route, including any state-border or permit requirements if you are leaving Tamil Nadu, and about the documents the operator carries. If an operator is vague, that is information in itself.

### 10. How are delays and problems handled?
Ask what happens on a breakdown, a roadblock or bad weather. A good operator has a plain answer.

## What should I check about the trip itself?

- **Weather and season** — hill roads are slower in rain and mist.
- **Crowd dates** — festivals, weekends and school holidays need earlier starts.
- **Group needs** — older members, young children, motion sickness, early temple slots.
- **Timing** — a realistic start time with a buffer, especially for ghat roads.

## What documents and details should I keep ready?

- **The written quotation or booking message,** saved on your phone and shared with the group coordinator.
- **The operator's and driver's phone numbers,** saved in more than one phone.
- **A list of passengers** with phone numbers, in case someone needs to be reached during the day.
- **ID proof for each adult,** especially if you will cross a state border or stay overnight.
- **Payment receipts** for the advance and the balance.
- **The itinerary,** with the pickup time and meeting points.

Keeping these in one chat thread means anyone in the group can find them in a minute.

## Red flags

- A price quoted without asking about your trip.
- Refusal to put the quotation in writing.
- Pressure to pay a large advance immediately.
- Vague answers on inclusions, cancellation or the driver.
- Photographs that do not match the vehicle that arrives.
