## How do I plan a family or group trip from Dindigul?

Plan it in six steps:

1. **Fix the who and when.** Count everyone including children and older members; agree dates (and a backup date).
2. **Pick the destination and trip length.** One day, one night or more.
3. **Decide how to travel.** One vehicle or several; read the options below.
4. **Collect the details.** Pickup point, start time, luggage, any special needs.
5. **Enquire once, clearly.** Send the details to the operator in a single message.
6. **Confirm in writing.** Quotation, inclusions, timing and terms.

## How should we prepare for the day?

- **Start early.** For hill trips especially, an early start gives you cooler hours and a daylight return.
- **Share one itinerary** with everyone — timings, pickup point, meeting point after each stop.
- **Pack for the weather:** light jackets for Kodaikanal, water and snacks for all trips, motion-sickness remedies if needed.
- **Keep a buffer** for weekends, festival days and school holidays.

## Who in the group should do what?

Group trips go smoothly when the jobs are shared:

- **One coordinator** who talks to the vehicle operator and holds the final plan.
- **One person for each family** who counts heads at every stop.
- **One person for documents and money,** so that ID proofs and payments are not scattered.
- **One person for the elders,** who makes sure they are comfortable, seated first and not hurried.

Write this down in the group chat. It takes two minutes and prevents most of the usual confusion.

## What if the group has different ideas about the plan?

Most groups do. Three rules help:

1. **Decide the non-negotiables first** — the one place everyone must see, and the latest acceptable return time.
2. **Choose by the least energetic person's pace.** The plan has to work for them, or it does not work.
3. **Agree a fallback** for rain or delay, so that a change of plan is not an argument.
