Dindigul and Oddanchatram do not have an airport of their own, so most visitors arrive by air at a nearby city and then travel by road — or arrive by train at **Dindigul Junction**. For one or two travellers a taxi is simple. For a family, a wedding party or a tour group, the plan needs a little more thought. This guide covers the options and how to avoid the usual problems.

*Distances are approximate and drawn from public travel sources; they vary by route and starting point. Check your exact route before you travel.*

## Which airport is closest?

- **Madurai Airport** is the nearest to Dindigul, at roughly **60–65 km**. It is usually the best choice.
- **Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) Airport** is roughly **90–100 km** from Dindigul.
- **Coimbatore Airport** is farther — sources give roughly **125–160 km** depending on route and destination — and may suit travellers heading to the Palani side.

From Dindigul, **Oddanchatram is about 29 km** further along the road toward Palani, and Palani is roughly 28 km beyond that. Add this to your timing if your final stop is Oddanchatram.

## Arriving by train

**Dindigul Junction** is a major railway station and a convenient arrival point if you are travelling by train. A group that arrives at the station can be met outside and continue by road to Oddanchatram, Palani, Kodaikanal or Sirumalai without changing vehicles.

## What to plan for a group transfer

- **Flight or train details.** Share the flight number, arrival time and station, so pickup timing can adjust to delays.
- **Number of passengers and luggage.** Groups with many bags need a vehicle with proper luggage space — tell us what you carry.
- **Arrival hour.** Late-night arrivals on hill or highway roads need extra care; mention the time up front.
- **Waiting time.** Allow time for baggage claim and boarding passes. Don't plan a tight temple or wedding schedule right after landing.
- **Final address.** Share the full destination address or a landmark for hotels, halls and guest houses, since not all are easy to find.
