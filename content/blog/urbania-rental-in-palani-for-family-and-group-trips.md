## Why do groups in Palani choose a single large vehicle?

On a temple day the practical problems are rarely the temple itself. They are parking, crowds, heat, and keeping a group — often including children and older members — together. A single Force Urbania helps because:

- **Everyone arrives and leaves together**, with one agreed meeting point afterwards.
- **Bags, water and spare clothes stay in one place** rather than being carried around.
- **Parking is one problem, not several** — one vehicle waits while you are at the temple, instead of several cars each looking for a space.
- **The group plan stays flexible** — if the queue is long you can adjust the timing of the next stop with one phone call.

## What should I consider when choosing a vehicle for group travel from Palani?

Think about these five things before you enquire:

1. **Who is travelling.** Older members and young children change how many stops you need and how early you start.
2. **How much luggage.** A temple day needs little; a Kodaikanal overnight needs more.
3. **Festival and weekend timing.** Roads around Palani fill up with walking pilgrims and extra traffic on festival days; buffer time matters more than speed.
4. **Hill roads.** If Kodaikanal is on the plan, think about comfort on the ghat road, especially for anyone prone to motion sickness.
5. **What the quotation covers.** Ask what is included and what is extra before you confirm.

## What are the practical tips for a Palani group day?

- **Pick one meeting point and tell everyone twice.** Crowds make it easy to lose people.
- **Save the vehicle's contact number** in every adult's phone, not just one person's.
- **Plan food around the visit.** Eating before the queue keeps children patient; a late lunch afterwards is easier than a rushed one.
- **Keep the return time flexible.** Temple visits run long, so leave a gap before the next commitment.
- **Respect the temple rules** on dress, footwear and phones, and share them with the group before you leave home.
