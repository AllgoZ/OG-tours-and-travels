Family trips are wonderful, and a little planning makes them smoother for everyone — especially when there are children or older relatives travelling. Use this checklist as a starting point and adapt it to your family.

## Two weeks before

- **Agree the dates and destination** with everyone travelling.
- **Confirm your vehicle** and share your group size and luggage so the quote is accurate.
- **Plan the rough route**, including where you will stop to rest and eat.
- Note any **bookings you need** at the destination, such as stays or entry tickets.

## A few days before

- Check that everyone's **ID and any required documents** are in order.
- Prepare a **small medical kit**: regular medicines, basics for motion sickness, plasters.
- Charge devices and prepare **entertainment for children** — downloaded shows, games, a favourite toy.
- Confirm the **pickup time and point** with everyone in the group.

## Packing for comfort

Keep a single "reach bag" in the cabin with the things you will want on the move:

- Water and simple snacks.
- Wipes, tissues and a small rubbish bag.
- Light layers for air-conditioning.
- Chargers and headphones.

Everything else can go in the main luggage. The less you have to dig around for during the drive, the calmer the journey.

> With children, the trick is to make stops part of the plan rather than interruptions to it.

## On the day of travel

- Have everyone ready a little before pickup time.
- Do a final check for phones, wallets, chargers and medicines.
- Keep the first stop relatively early so younger travellers can stretch.
- Share the route and contact details within the group.

## Keeping everyone comfortable

Travelling together in one vehicle keeps the family together and makes the journey part of the holiday rather than a chore. Build in regular breaks, keep water handy, and do not over-pack the day's itinerary — a relaxed pace is what most families remember fondly.
