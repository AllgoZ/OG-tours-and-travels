## What details should I share when I enquire?

A good enquiry gets a good, accurate reply. Send these in one message:

1. **Number of travellers**, including children and older members.
2. **Pickup point in Dindigul** (area, landmark or Google Maps pin) and the drop point.
3. **Dates and timing** — the day you leave and the day you return, or "one-day trip".
4. **Destinations** — for example Kodaikanal, Palani, Madurai or a temple circuit.
5. **Luggage** — a rough idea of bags, since it affects how the vehicle is used.
6. **Anything special** — a wedding function, an early-morning darshan, an older passenger who needs an easy seat.

With this we can tell you honestly whether the Force Urbania is the right vehicle for your group, or whether your plan needs adjusting.

## What should I confirm before I pay?

Before you confirm any vehicle booking, ask for these points in writing. They apply to us and to anyone else you speak to:

- **Exactly what the quotation includes** — and what is extra (tolls, parking, night halt, waiting time, hill-road charges if any).
- **Pickup and drop timing**, and what happens if the schedule changes on the day.
- **Seating and luggage space** for your group size.
- **Driver and contact details** a day before the trip.
- **The cancellation and advance policy.**

We have put a longer version of this list in [things to check before booking an Urbania](/blog/things-to-check-before-booking-an-urbania).
