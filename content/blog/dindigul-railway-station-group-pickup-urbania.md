**Dindigul Junction** is one of the main railway stations in this part of Tamil Nadu, and many groups use it as the start of their trip: a family arriving for a wedding, friends coming to visit, an office team beginning a tour of the hills. The train gets them to Dindigul. What happens next — who meets them, where, with how much luggage and how they travel on — is where plans go wrong. This guide explains how to arrange a **group pickup at Dindigul railway station** and how a **Force Urbania** from **OG Tours and Travels** can take the group onward.

*Train timings change and platform details vary, so confirm on the railway's official channels or your ticketing app. We do not publish prices.*

## Why use a private pickup instead of finding transport at the station?

A single traveller can find an auto or a cab. A group of eight with bags cannot easily. Arriving at a station with no confirmed vehicle means negotiation, splitting across several cars, and the first hour of the trip lost. A pre-arranged pickup means someone is waiting, the vehicle is the one you agreed on, and the group leaves together.

## What should I share with the operator?

1. **Train name and number**, arrival date and scheduled time.
2. **Number of travellers** and how many are children or elders.
3. **Luggage** — rough count and anything large.
4. **The destination** and the first stop — hotel, wedding hall, Kodaikanal, Palani.
5. **A phone number** of a person travelling who can answer on arrival.
6. **Any special needs**, such as a wheelchair.

Sharing the train number lets the pickup be planned around the actual arrival, not just the timetable.

## What if the train is late?

Trains often run late, so build the plan around that:

- Tell the operator the train number so the arrival can be tracked.
- Agree who will message the operator if the train is badly delayed.
- Do not plan a tight schedule right after arrival, such as a ceremony or a temple slot.
- Confirm how waiting time is handled in the quotation.

## Where do we meet?

Agree a meeting point at the station before you arrive, for example the main exit or a named spot, and share the driver's name and number. Large stations can be confusing after a long journey, and a clear meeting point saves a lot of phone calls. Parking and access around stations can be tight, so follow the driver's instruction on where the group should assemble.

## What about luggage?

Count the bags before you arrive and tell the operator, particularly if you carry large suitcases, gifts for a function or equipment. Luggage space is part of the fit, along with seating. ## Should I travel straight onto a hill road after a long train journey?

If possible, no. After an overnight train, passengers are tired and more sensitive to a winding road. Allow breakfast, a rest and a gentle start, especially for children and elders. If you must go straight on, tell the operator so that breaks can be planned.

## What should the quotation say?

- The **pickup location** and time, with a waiting policy.
- What happens in case of delay.
- What is **included** and what is **extra**.
- The **driver's contact**, before arrival.
