There is no single best time to visit Kodaikanal, only the best time for *your* group. Some families want cool weather and a clear sky. Others prefer fewer people and green hills. Some plan around a flower show. From Dindigul, the hill station is close enough to go in any season, which is both a blessing and a source of confusion. This guide sets out what each season is like, what it means for planning, and when to book a vehicle for a group trip with **OG Tours and Travels**.

*Sources disagree on exact months and temperatures, and weather varies from year to year. Treat this as a guide and check a current forecast before you go. We have given no prices.*

## When is the best time to visit Kodaikanal?

Most travel sources point to **November to February** for cool, pleasant weather, and to **March to June** as the main holiday season, with the annual flower show in May. The monsoon months are greener and quieter but wetter and mistier. Which season suits you depends on what you want from the trip.

## Winter (roughly November to February)

**What it is like.** Cool days and cold nights, with temperatures reported in the single digits to mid-teens at night in the coldest weeks, and occasional frost in December and January.

**Good for.** Clear days, comfortable walks, warm clothes and a cosy evening.

**Watch out for.** This is a busy period, particularly around Christmas, New Year and long weekends. Stays fill up and traffic builds. Mornings and evenings can be properly cold, so carry warm clothing for everyone, including small children and elders.

## Summer (roughly March to June)

**What it is like.** The hill station is a relief from the plains' heat. It is also the main holiday season, and the **Annual Horticulture Show** at Bryant Park is held in May, according to public sources.

**Good for.** Escaping the heat, visiting the flower show and school-holiday trips.

**Watch out for.** Crowds on the ghat, in town and at viewpoints. Parking near the lake is tight. Start early, and consider a weekday. Check the show dates locally, as they can change.

## Monsoon (roughly June to September)

**What it is like.** Green, misty and wet. Waterfalls such as the Silver Cascade are at their best; visibility can be poor.

**Good for.** Fewer visitors, lush scenery and lower demand for stays.

**Watch out for.** Rain and mist slow the hill drive, viewpoints may be hidden, and landslides sometimes disrupt roads. Keep a flexible plan and a backup date. Read [Kodaikanal ghat road tips for group travel](/blog/kodaikanal-ghat-road-tips-for-group-travel) before you go.

## Shoulder months

Late February to early March and late September to early October are suggested by some sources as a balance: moderate weather, fewer crowds. They are good choices if your dates are flexible.

## Which season suits which group?

| Group | Often a good fit |
|---|---|
| Families with young children | Winter days or shoulder months, with warm clothes |
| Older travellers | Shoulder months, avoiding peak crowds and heavy rain |
| School-holiday groups | Summer, booked early |
| Photographers and nature lovers | Monsoon or post-monsoon, with patience |
| Weekend visitors | Anytime — but book ahead |

## What should I plan around dates?

- **Long weekends and school holidays.** Book your vehicle and stay early.
- **Weekends in general.** The ghat and the lake are busier. Start earlier than you think you need to.
- **Weather.** Check the forecast two or three days before and again the evening before.
- **Festivals in Dindigul and Palani.** They affect road traffic on the approach roads; see the [guide to Palani temple trips](/blog/dindigul-to-palani-temple-trip-by-urbania) if your plan includes both.

## Can I decide between a day trip and an overnight stay by season?

Often, yes. In winter, days are short and evenings cold, so a night in the hills allows a daylight descent. In summer, crowds make an early start more important. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month to visit Kodaikanal?
It depends on your priority. November to February for cool weather, May for the flower show, monsoon months for greenery.

### Is Kodaikanal crowded in summer?
Yes. Expect traffic and busy viewpoints. Start early and avoid peak weekends if you can.

### Is Kodaikanal good in the monsoon?
It can be beautiful, but rain and mist slow travel and hide viewpoints. Keep a flexible plan.

### How cold does Kodaikanal get?
Winters can be cold, especially at night. Sources give a range of temperatures, so carry warm clothing and check the forecast.
